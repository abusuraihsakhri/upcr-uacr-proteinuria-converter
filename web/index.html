Interpretation notes
    Clinical interpretation

    A1: UACR <30 mg/g; A2: 30–299 mg/g; A3: ≥300 mg/g. Results are not a diagnosis or a treatment directive. Confirm persistent albuminuria as clinically indicated.

    Method: Analyte (mg/dL) ÷ creatinine (mg/dL) × 1,000 = mg/g creatinine. Multiply mg/g by 0.11312 to obtain mg/mmol. Estimated mg/day = mg/g × independently supplied creatinine g/day.

    Limitations: Spot ratios are affected by biological variability and creatinine excretion. UPCR cannot be converted to UACR without measured albumin or additional assumptions. Do not use the derived 24-hour estimate as a substitute for timed urine collection when indicated.

    Sources: KDIGO CKD guideline · NIDDK urine albumin assessment
